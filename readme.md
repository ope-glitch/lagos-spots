# Lagos Spots

## Product Requirement Document (PRD)

### Overview
Lagos Spots is a tourism and dining discovery website designed to help visitors explore the best cultural, scenic, and culinary experiences in Lagos, Nigeria. The platform showcases popular attractions, vibrant neighborhoods, waterfront experiences, and restaurants that define the city's energy and lifestyle.

### Goal
To connect locals, tourists, and business travelers with memorable places to visit and foods to enjoy across Lagos using a simple, informative, and visually appealing listing platform.

### Target Audience
- Tourists visiting Lagos for the first time
- Local residents looking for new experiences
- Food lovers and event seekers
- Business travelers seeking short leisure escapes
- Families and groups planning city outings

### Core Features
- Browse tourism destinations by category
- Discover popular restaurants, cafes, and street food hubs
- View key details such as location, hours, ambiance, and specialties
- Filter by area, budget, and attraction type
- Learn about Lagos culture, nightlife, beaches, and landmarks

### Tourism Spot Listings
1. Lekki Conservation Centre
   A protected natural reserve offering a long canopy walkway, nature trails, and beautiful views of Lagos' green landscape. Ideal for eco-tourism, photography, and outdoor activities.

2. Tarkwa Bay Beach
   A relaxing island beach known for calm waters, scenic coastline, and water sports. It is a favorite destination for day trips, swimming, and beachside picnics.

3. National Museum Lagos
   A cultural landmark that highlights Nigerian history, arts, and heritage through exhibits, artifacts, and historical collections.

4. Freedom Park
   A historic public space built around a former colonial prison, now transformed into a vibrant cultural and recreational center for events, music, and social gatherings.

5. Lekki Arts and Crafts Market
   A lively marketplace where visitors can shop for Nigerian art, handmade crafts, clothing, accessories, and souvenirs.

6. Third Mainland Bridge Viewpoints
   A popular city experience offering panoramic vistas of Lagos skyline, especially at sunset and evening hours.

7. Nike Art Gallery
   One of the most iconic art spaces in Lagos, showcasing contemporary and traditional Nigerian art, fashion, and creative culture.

8. Badagry Heritage Museum
   A historical destination that tells the story of the region's colonial past, migration history, and cultural evolution.

### Dining Spot Listings
1. Yellow Chilli
   A well-known restaurant serving Nigerian and international dishes in a lively atmosphere. Popular for family dinners, celebrations, and casual gatherings.

2. The Yellow Cafe
   A stylish café with a relaxed ambience that offers coffee, sweet treats, and light meals suitable for brunch or casual meetings.

3. Bungalow Restaurant
   A modern dining destination known for its delicious seafood, grilled options, and welcoming environment for relaxed meals.

4. Terra Kulture
   More than a restaurant, this venue combines fine dining, art, and cultural experiences. It is ideal for guests who want good food and live cultural performances.

5. Shiro Restaurant & Bar
   A premium dining and nightlife spot that offers Asian-inspired cuisine, cocktails, and a stylish urban atmosphere.

6. The View Restaurant
   A scenic dining location offering a blend of comfort food, sunset views, and a polished restaurant experience.

7. Calabash Bistro
   Known for a rich mix of Nigerian and international flavors, with a warm ambiance that appeals to both casual diners and special occasions.

8. Street Food Hubs in Yaba and VI
   These vibrant food districts feature local favorites such as suya, puff-puff, jollof rice, grilled fish, and small chops, bringing Lagos' culinary culture to life.

### Brand Personality
The brand should feel energetic, modern, and culturally rich. It should celebrate Lagos as a city of beauty, music, diversity, and unforgettable experiences while promoting ease of discovery for every visitor.

### Suggested Website Experience
- Hero section with Lagos skyline, beaches, and food imagery
- Search and filters for travelers and diners
- Featured listings with ratings, prices, and area tags
- City guides for neighborhoods like Lekki, Victoria Island, Yaba, and Ikeja
- “What to do today” and “Where to eat tonight” sections

### Conclusion
Lagos Spots should become the go-to digital guide for discovering the city's most exciting tourism landmarks and dining experiences. By blending cultural discovery with culinary exploration, the website helps people experience the best of Lagos in one place.
